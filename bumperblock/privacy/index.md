## What we collect

Bumper Block collects anonymous gameplay statistics (which levels you play, how runs end,
which upgrades you buy) and basic device information (model, operating system version,
app version) through Unity Analytics, to improve the game.

## What we don't collect

No personal data, contacts, location or advertising identifiers are collected, and nothing
is shared with third parties for advertising.

## Feedback you send

When you send a note from the game's Send feedback screen or the feedback page on this site,
we receive what you typed and, unless you switch it off on that screen, your phone's model
and Android version, the game's version, the level you were on and a one-line summary of your
last run. A contact detail is only sent if you type one. Notes go to a form and spreadsheet we
keep for the testing period, and are never shared or used for advertising.

## Your choices

You can turn collection off at any time in the Garage under Settings.

## How long we keep it

Data is kept for 12 months.
